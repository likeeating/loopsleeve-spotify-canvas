# Loopsleeve: Turn an album cover into a Spotify Canvas loop

A Spotify Canvas needs a short 9:16 looping video that starts and ends on your artwork. [https://loopsleeve.com/](https://loopsleeve.com/) is an AI Spotify Canvas generator from our team at Deep Origin. Upload your album cover and get a seamless 4 to 7 second 9:16 MP4 loop sized for Spotify Canvas.

## What it does

- Turns your album cover into a seamless 9:16 looping video that starts and ends on your artwork
- 4 to 7 second loop, sized for Spotify Canvas
- Pick a motion style
- Preview the 9:16 framing
- Download a 720p MP4 for Spotify for Artists

## How to make a Canvas

1. Upload your album cover.
2. Pick a motion style and preview the 9:16 framing.
3. Generate the loop.
4. Download the 720p MP4 for Spotify for Artists.
